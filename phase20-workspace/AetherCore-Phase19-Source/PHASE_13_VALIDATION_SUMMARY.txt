PHASE 13 RELIABILITY CLOSURE
- Shared collector runtime with monotonic deadlines, hierarchical cancellation, watchdog panic containment and IsolationGate quarantine.
- Finite semisynchronous WMI enumeration with explicit WBEM_S_TIMEDOUT / WBEM_S_FALSE handling and fail-closed result-count validation.
- Direct storage IOCTL caller watchdogs with quarantine semantics; no claim of forced termination for arbitrary blocked vendor-driver calls.
- Byte-level ATA SMART and NVMe health parsers with returned-byte, offset, overlap and truncation validation.
- Structured Windows Event Log rendering through EvtRender system/user value contexts, bounded buffers/property counts and immediate RAII ownership of returned event handles.
- Minidump partial-failure isolation and permission-denied classification.
- Typed provider faults carried through diagnostics/Protobuf/UI while raw technical detail is not rendered in normal UI surfaces.
- Provider fault detail is UTF-8 safely bounded to a final maximum of 4 KiB before snapshot/journal/IPC transport.
- Diagnostic provider/supervisor/spawn/persistence failures finalize visibly instead of silently leaving Collecting or claiming durable history.
